About Braniomngima Hospital

Healthcare Close to the Community

Braniomngima Hospital is a healthcare facility located in Oyugis, Kenya, along the Kisii–Kisumu Highway, opposite Equity Bank.

The hospital is positioned to provide accessible healthcare services to individuals and families within Oyugis and the surrounding communities.

Braniomngima Hospital serves individuals and families in Oyugis and surrounding communities, providing accessible healthcare in a convenient location along the Kisii–Kisumu Highway.
