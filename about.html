Who We Are

Braniongima Hospital

Braniongima Hospital is a healthcare facility conveniently located along the Kisii–Kisumu Highway in Oyugis, opposite Equity Bank.

We are committed to providing a welcoming healthcare environment for patients and families seeking medical care in Oyugis and the surrounding area.

More information about our history, leadership, medical team and areas of specialization will be added as part of the hospital's official website information.
